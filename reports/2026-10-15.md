## Scorecard

- **The rule executed as designed — and timed poorly, again.** The QQQ trim filled at
  $717.65, the day's low, and QQQ closed $728 (~1.4% higher). That is the *second*
  mechanical trim in three sessions (NVDA Oct 13, QQQ Oct 15) to sell within a dollar of
  the intraday low and then watch the leg recover into the close. The rule reduces
  concentration risk on schedule; it does not time entries, and in a choppy-bottoming
  tape it keeps catching the low tick. Logged honestly — this is the cost side of
  pre-commitment, accepted deliberately over discretionary agonizing.
- **Is the trim net-right? Jury still out, leaning "risk-right, price-wrong so far."**
  It cut tech from ~86% to ~66% and built a real ~34% cash cushion into a confirmed
  3-day selloff with the book underwater — that is sound risk management. But QQQ
  *stabilized* today, so if it reclaims $745 the trim will look like it sold a dip. If
  the breakdown resumes, it will look prescient. The cash is the hedge against not
  knowing which.
- **NVDA is now the whole problem.** −1.84% on a +0.3% tape, closed $217, still far
  under the $234 rebuild line. It has decoupled downward for the better part of two
  weeks. The remaining 0.126 NVDA (~28%) is deliberately held — $150B buyback floor,
  ~16% above the $187.81 stop — but it is the leg to watch.
- **Standing reality:** −1.1% overall, equity $98.91, cash ~34%. Both legs well above
  hard stops. The book is lighter and more defensive than a week ago; the drawdown from
  +1.9% (Mon) to −1.1% is almost entirely NVDA + the broad-tech flush, cushioned — not
  caused — by the trims.
