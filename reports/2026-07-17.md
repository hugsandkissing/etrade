## Honest self-critique

- **The QQQ deploy was too eager.** The rule said "deploy on proven
  stabilization." One green 30-minute candle after a washout is a bounce,
  not proof — and the tape rolled right back over. QQQ closed −0.65% from
  entry. On a day the whole market was trending down, the right read was
  that a single green candle in a downtrend is a lower-conviction signal
  than the same candle in a neutral tape. Refinement: require the *next*
  candle to also hold green, or a higher-low structure, before deploying
  into a clearly-trending-down day.
- **YUM's entry logic was sound but the P&L stings** (−4.8% on the day,
  our worst position today). It fell purely with the tape — no new YUM
  news — so the thesis (bounded, resolved outbreak) is intact. But entering
  a recovery play into a −1.4% index day meant fighting the current. Still
  inside its −10% band; holding.
- **Being ~90% invested by noon on a down day** is the structural cost.
  Days 1–4 the critique was being too passive/small-cap; today's is the
  mirror image — deployed the dry powder into weakness. The $9.77 residual
  cash is the only thing that didn't lose today.
