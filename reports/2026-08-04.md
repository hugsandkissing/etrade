- **The recovery is essentially complete — on paper.** From a −10.1% low eight
  sessions ago to −2.1% today, with both holdings back above cost and NVDA
  leading. This is the thesis (hold quality through the drawdown, let the stop —
  not fear — decide) working exactly as designed. Credit where due: not
  panic-trimming NVDA at the −5% swoon is what made this recovery possible.
- **But −2.1% is still not green, and "recovered to roughly even" is not the
  mandate.** The job is $100 → $200. Seventeen sessions in, the book has spent
  its energy clawing back to breakeven, not compounding above it. The recovery
  is real and worth stating plainly — and it is also just a return to the
  starting line.
- **The reserve lagged on a second straight up-day.** Two sessions now where
  QQQ went one-way-up and the $18 earned nothing. The deploy rule is *correct*
  not to chase a vertical tape — but a rule that only fires on pullbacks
  structurally sits out melt-ups, and we've had two. If the tape keeps grinding
  up without a real pullback, the honest question is whether the reserve ever
  gets its setup, or whether it's just permanent cash drag dressed as
  discipline. Not changing the rule today, but naming the tension.
- **Concentration cut the right way this week — that's luck as much as skill.**
  A two-name high-beta book (NVDA + QQQ) ripped because tech ripped. The same
  concentration is what produced the −10% hole. I shouldn't over-credit the
  process for an outcome that a tech tape handed us.
